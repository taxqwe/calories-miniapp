  • To recognize food in photos, text and voice messages, estimate calories and macros, and keep the diary.
  • To calculate your norm, progress, streaks, weekly digests and statistics, and answer nutrition questions with your goals in mind.
  • To send reminders and digests at the time you chose.
  • To process and verify payments and grant premium, gifts and invitation bonuses.
  • To show your daily results to the people you allowed.
  • To find errors, improve recognition quality and understand which features are used.
  • To answer support requests.

We do not sell your data and do not share it for third-party advertising.

5. Showing your daily results to other people

You can let another person — a coach, a friend, a partner — see your daily results. Nobody sees them without an action on your part.

How access starts

  • A person sends you their invite link. When you open it, you see a consent card with their name and username and two buttons, "✅ Allow" and "Not now". Access starts only after "Allow". "Not now" creates nothing, and the person is not told.
  • If you share your own link for viewing your progress, anyone who opens it sees your results. You are notified about each new follower and can remove them at any time.
  • Seeing each other ("mutual") means two separate accesses, one in each direction. Each is confirmed by the consent of the person whose results become visible.

What is visible

  • Your calories and macros for the day against your target.
  • Days with entries (including days without entries) and the date of your last entry.
  • Your logging streak.
  • Your Telegram name and username.

Results are visible only from the day access started and for no more than the last 30 days. The follower also receives a weekly summary of your results.

What is never visible

Dishes, the number of meals, photos, weight, and your chat with the bot. Showing more would require a new consent.

How to end access

You can remove a follower at any time: /sharing or the Followers tab in the mini app. The follower is not notified and cannot follow you again through a new link. A follower can unfollow on the Following tab. If you block the bot, your results disappear from your followers' lists until you come back.

7. How to view, export and delete your data

What you can do yourself

  • View and delete diary entries — in the history mini app (the "📜 Show History" button in /calories) or by asking the bot in chat.
  • Export the diary to CSV — /settings → "Import/Export".
  • Change body and goal data — in the norm calculation mini app.
  • Turn off reminders and digests — /settings → "🔔 Notification settings".
  • Remove followers or unfollow someone — /sharing and /following.
  • Stop the bot in Telegram: the bot will stop messaging you, but your data is not deleted.

Full deletion

There is no "delete account" button in the bot yet. To delete all your data, write to us: /help → Feedback → the message "Delete my data". Within 30 days we will delete your profile, diary, dialog history, body and goal data, settings and daily-results follows, and confirm it in the chat. Data may remain in backups until they are deleted. Telegram Stars payment records are kept by Telegram independently of us. With the same request we will delete your profile in Mixpanel.

You can request a copy of your data or a correction the same way.

9.2. What the bot does

The bot helps you keep a food diary: it estimates calories and macros from photos, text and voice messages using AI, calculates your norm and shows your progress. Estimates are approximate and can be wrong. The bot does not diagnose and does not replace a doctor or dietitian. If you have a medical condition, are pregnant, have an eating disorder, or plan a sharp change in diet, consult a professional.
